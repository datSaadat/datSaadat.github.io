Student mentoring

Mentored undergraduate students on projects involving:

  1. Oxide memristor fabrication

    Fabrication of oxide-based memristors using sputter deposition.

  2. Device characterization

    Electrical characterization of fabricated memristors and analysis of negative differential resistance (NDR) associated with the metal–insulator transition in VO₂.

  3. Flexible memristors

    Fabrication and electrical characterization of flexible memristors using spin-coated PEDOT:Tos films.

  4. Computer vision inference simulation

    Evaluation of CNN inference, including ResNet architectures and ImageNet benchmarks, using DNN+NeuroSim V2.0 and CrossSim.
