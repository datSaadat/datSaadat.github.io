GOOD TO KNOW

Made for your memories.

Signed-in records are stored in your configured Firebase project. Access rules must restrict each account to its own records. Records are not end-to-end encrypted.

Demo mode uses fictional sample travels and unencrypted browser storage. It is separate from your account and never uploads automatically. Export your records before changing devices or clearing browser storage.

Maps show countries and territories using Natural Earth boundaries. They are illustrative, not a statement about sovereignty. Small islands may not appear at this scale; use the country selector. US state totals exclude DC and territories. Nearby cities are never inferred from a park visit.

World map: Natural Earth via datasets/geo-countries. State geometry: Natural Earth. The city and park catalog is a starter selection; custom entry is supported.
